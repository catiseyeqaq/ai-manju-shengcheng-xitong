## 4. 大师_03 每镜提示词包（C-3）

结构：`固定核心` + `本镜动作` + `Audio`。  
首尾帧：用大师_01/02 产出图；无尾帧需求时可只接首帧（若节点允许），但建议每镜都做首+尾。

### 镜 01
**动作：**
```
[Shot 1] First-person POV seated in Chinese classroom. Soft afternoon sun. Girl sits ahead by the window, quietly taking notes, then slightly turns her head; hair catches the light. Slow gentle push-in, small amplitude at slow speed.
```
**Audio：**
```
overall_soundscape: quiet classroom, page turning, distant teacher voice in Chinese tone (no clear words).
non_diegetic_music: very soft warm piano, low volume.
```

### 镜 02
```
[Shot 1] First-person POV looking forward. Teacher at the front. The girl senses the gaze, briefly looks back toward the camera, freezes a beat, gives a tiny smile, then turns forward again. Stable frame, warm natural light, Simplified Chinese on blackboard.
```
**Audio：** teacher murmur, chalk, soft ambient; music barely there.

### 镜 03
```
[Shot 1] Class-end bell rings. POV hands packing books. The girl passes by and gently taps the desk edge with her book, looks back to signal "let's go", light playful motion. Small follow.
```
**Audio：** school bell, chairs, light chatter; no dialogue.

### 镜 04
```
[Shot 1] POV walking Chinese school corridor. Girl walks ahead, looks back while talking casually with a light smile. Bulletin boards in Simplified Chinese only. Gentle tracking, small amplitude at slow speed.
```
**Audio：** footsteps, corridor reverb, distant student voices.

### 镜 05
```
[Shot 1] Outside near teaching building. Light playful moment: girl steps back half a step laughing, raises a hand toward camera as if blocking, then looks back smiling. Keep motion small and soft, not chaotic.
```
**Audio：** laughter, wind, soft campus ambience.

### 镜 06
```
[Shot 1] At Chinese high school gate at sunset. Students leaving in background. Girl stands half a step ahead, turns slightly, shyly asks in Chinese: <d>[Chinese] 要不要一起回家？</d> Soft push-in.
```
**Audio：** after-school crowd, distant traffic; music gentle swell.

### 镜 07
```
[Shot 1] Walking home side by side on Chinese residential street. Shop signs Simplified Chinese only. Girl occasionally turns to say small everyday things. Stable forward move, golden hour, hair in wind.
```
**Audio：** footsteps, city bed, light breeze.

### 镜 08
```
[Shot 1] Same street, mood shifts. Girl becomes quiet, grips backpack strap, eyes avoid camera, ear tips and cheeks faintly blush. Slow the pace. Anticipation before confession.
```
**Audio：** footsteps dominant; city bed lowered; music thins.

### 镜 09 ★关键
```
[Shot 1] She stops; POV stops. Facing her. She looks down, breathes, then looks up into camera, blushing, nervous but sincere, says in Chinese: <d>[Chinese] 其实……我喜欢你很久了。</d> Slow push-in, sunset on her face.
```
**Audio：** near silence + far city; soft emotional underscore enters after the line.

### 镜 10
```
[Shot 1] After confession pause. POV steps half a step closer. She presses her lips, then gives a relieved shy smile, sunset backlight, hair in breeze. Hold on her expectant expression as ending.
```
**Audio：** wind, distant city; music resolves softly.
